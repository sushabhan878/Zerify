# Brag Plan: Zerify

## What is this app?
Zerify is a direct brand–creator collaboration platform for finding creators, sending briefs, managing campaigns, tracking reach, and automating payouts without agency overhead.

## The angle
Make Zerify feel like the shortcut between a brand idea and a live creator campaign: the old agency maze gets compressed into one smooth purple interface. The film moves from a bold promise into the actual product flow — discover a creator, send a brief, watch campaign performance, then land on the Zerify mark.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Oversized type snaps in: “Goodbye, agency maze.” A thin purple route line draws from BRAND to CREATOR while the background grid breathes. The promise is instantly readable and visually specific to Zerify’s direct-connect positioning.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real Zerify hero UI slides into view with the verbatim promise “Brands & Influencers to Connect.”
- A creator discovery card appears with “AI Creator Match,” “92% Match,” “24.8K reach,” and “8.7% engagement,” then the “Invite to Campaign” action gets a tactile click.
- A campaign workspace/analytics panel resolves into “Creative analytics,” “68% outperform,” and “Automate payouts,” turning the product promise into a working flow.

## Outro / punchline
The interface panels collapse into the Zerify logo and the line “Direct connection. Zero agency overhead.” A final “Get started for free” capsule holds long enough to feel like a launch card, not a fleeting end frame.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → search/discover a creator → send a brief/invite → monitor campaign reach and analytics → automate payout.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: smooth, high-energy product sizzle with premium purple motion
- Interpretation: Fast cuts and punchy reveals, but every claim holds long enough to read; motion stays polished with glass panels, clean wipes, spring arrivals, and restrained SFX.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24.5 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #07090E
- Accent: #A855F7 with #F472B6 and #818CF8 as supporting glow tones
- Text: #F3F4F6 / #FFFFFF
- Display font: Playfair Display
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Strongest visual element: the dark glassmorphic hero/dashboard UI with dotted grid, purple glow, creator image, and match/campaign cards

## Share copy (draft)
Zerify turns the brand–creator maze into one direct flow: discover, brief, measure, pay. Built for campaigns without the agency overhead.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer with polished interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` — upbeat, punchy 109.96 BPM
- Music treatment: start at 0.15s with a 0.35s fade-in, sit around 0.30 volume, and taper over the final 1.0s; favor strong cues around 15.82s and 20.19s for the analytics reveal and logo lockup
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `.agents/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; beat-grid windows at 3.01–4.10s for hook/reveal, 8.73–10.38s for creator cards, 13.64–15.82s for dashboard motion, and 20.19–22.92s for logo/outro
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the hero glow and the final logo halo breathe with pre-extracted low-band energy. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate and motion-matched; clean clicks for UI action, soft impacts for scene changes, one brighter final hit
- Audio-coupled moments: route line draw, creator card stagger, invite click, analytics counter landing, logo reveal
- Restraint rule: no constant clicking under every text entrance and no SFX louder than the music’s major landing beat.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Direct connection — 3.4s
Dark Zerify grid with purple/pink glows. “GOODBYE, AGENCY MAZE.” slams in, followed by a route line connecting BRAND to CREATOR. Small label: “ZERIFY / EARLY ACCESS”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — route line draws left-to-right, then the two endpoint labels lock in.
Audio intent: quick, confident opening hit.
Audio-coupled idea: line draw and endpoint chips snap near the first beat window.
Music: upbeat, immediate.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The platform — 4.1s
The real `zerify-hero.png` UI scales into a framed window. Overlaid copy reads “The fastest & easiest way for Brands & Influencers to connect.” A slim “Get started for free” capsule rises beneath.
Sequential/interaction: yes — frame arrives, headline settles, CTA capsule follows.
Audio intent: open the product world with a smooth lift.
Audio-coupled idea: hero card lands on a soft impact, CTA gets a light click.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Find the right creator — 4.4s
Split frame: creator discovery UI on the right using the real `ChatGPT Image Jul 29, 2026, 10_52_30 PM.png` asset, with left-side copy “Discover creators that fit the brief.” Match chips arrive one by one: “92% Match”, “24.8K Reach”, “8.7% Engagement”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three metric chips arrive in readable stagger; a cursor moves to “Invite to Campaign”.
Audio intent: rising, tactile, more momentum.
Audio-coupled idea: chip arrivals land on every other beat; invite button clicks at the end.
Transition mood: hard-cut with directional slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Run the campaign — 4.8s
The campaign workspace appears as a glass panel over the dotted field. Left copy: “Briefs in. Campaigns moving.” Right-side panel highlights “Creative analytics”, “68% outperform”, and a live reach line.
Sequential/interaction: yes — brief row, analytics card, and reach number resolve in that order.
Audio intent: confident proof beat.
Audio-coupled idea: analytics number ticks up and the line chart draws on the 15.82s strong cue.
Transition mood: smooth zoom → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Direct flow — 3.8s
Three connected pills sweep across the frame: “DISCOVER” → “BRIEF” → “MEASURE”. A green payout confirmation card appears: “Payout automated ✓”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — pills move as one route; payout card pops after the route completes.
Audio intent: payoff, clean and satisfying.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft confirmation ding at the green card.
Transition mood: bright flash cut → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Zerify — 4.0s
The route, cards, and glows collapse toward the center. Zerify logo appears with “Direct connection. Zero agency overhead.” and a final “Get started for free” capsule.
Sequential/interaction: yes — logo first, tagline second, CTA last; then hold.
Audio intent: celebratory but controlled final lockup.
Audio-coupled idea: logo landing near 20.19s strong cue, final SFX ring over the music fade.
Transition mood: final hold
