# Brag Plan: Zerify

## What is this app?
Zerify is an enterprise-grade creator-brand collaboration platform that eliminates ad agency overhead by connecting brands directly with vetted influencers for briefs, automated escrow payouts, campaign workspace, and real-time ROAS analytics.

## The angle
Creator marketing is notoriously bogged down by 40% agency fees, opaque rosters, and manual invoicing chaos. Zerify flips the script: verified creator discovery with 98% audience fit confidence, automated milestones with escrow protection, and live performance metrics — delivered in an ultra-sleek, glassmorphic dark interface.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An elegant, high-contrast dark statement that cuts straight to the pain point:
"Traditional creator marketing is broken by 40% agency markups."
Followed by the dramatic sonic impact and glowing violet lightning reveal.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **AI Creator Match & Discovery**: Pinpoint vetted influencers across Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok with a 98% audience fit score.
- **Direct Campaign Workspace & Escrow**: Structured deliverables, automatic contract generation, and fraud-free escrow milestone releases.
- **Real-Time Analytics Dashboard**: Continuous live tracking of impressions, engagement rates, verified reach, and real-time ROAS.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero agency overhead. Pure performance."
Zerify logo with glowing conic aura and clear CTA: "Get started for free at zerify.com".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Discover & Match**: Filter top creators with verified follower engagement and 98% audience fit score.
2. **Collaborate & Escrow**: Launch briefs, review proposals, and lock payments safely in escrow.
3. **Analyze & Payout**: Track live ROI analytics and trigger instant automated bank payouts upon deliverable signoff.
