# Brand Discovery PRD (brand_discovery.md)

## Product

**Zerify -- Brand Discovery Module**

## Vision

Enable influencers to proactively discover, evaluate, and connect with
brands that are the best fit for their audience. The experience should
feel less like browsing a directory and more like having an AI-powered
business development assistant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goals

-   Help creators discover relevant brands.
-   Increase creator-brand collaborations.
-   Reduce time spent searching.
-   Improve outreach success using AI recommendations.
-   Build long-term creator-brand relationships.
