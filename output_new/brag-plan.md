# Brag Plan: Zerify

## What is this app?
Zerify is a brand ↔ influencer marketplace: brands post campaigns with escrow-protected budgets, creators get AI match-scored to them, and everything — briefs, deliverables, payouts — runs inside one dashboard.

## The angle
Play up the "match made in heaven" energy with a full-speed hype edit. The product's signature feature is the **AI Match Score** (creators are scored 86–97% against brands), and its most reassuring claim is **escrow-protected payouts** ("See exactly who you are hiring before any money moves"). The video treats finding a creator like a match-win: lightning-fast cuts, score counters, cards dealt, money landing safely.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen → giant italic serif line slams in on the first strong beat: "Brands swipe right on 10,000 creators." Then immediately: "Wrong ones burn budget." — setting up the match pain in one beat.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **AI Match Score counter** — a card slams in and the number counts 0 → 97% with a purple glow, "97% MATCH" badge stamped on the downbeat.
2. **Escrow lock moment** — "₹2,33,800" value + a lock glyph; line from the product: "Money doesn't move until work does." (echo of "See exactly who you are hiring before any money moves").
3. **Real product screenshots** — the actual AI Creator Match dashboard and Zerify hero shot with a Ken Burns punch-in, proving the thing is real.

## Outro / punchline
Logo + "ZERIFY" slams in on the strongest cue with "The fastest way for brands & creators to connect." + CTA chip "Get started for free" (the site's real CTA). Hold on the lockup.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (campaign discovery card with match %) → key action (accept/verify the match) → result (escrow-funded campaign, payout secured). Recreated as: match card deal → 97% counter + verify click → escrow confirmation + campaign value.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic (mapped from "high pace, crazy animations")
- Creative direction: high-pace hype reel — hard cuts, zoom cuts, counters, cards dealt at speed; loud but on-brand (Zerify's own dark/purple/pink glassmorphic world)
- Interpretation: 6-8 rapid scenes, some under 2s; ALL-CAPS accents; hard cuts and flash cuts; but text always holds long enough to read (fast-in, then hold).

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 28 seconds (user asked 20-30s; chaotic pacing needs room for 8 scenes)

- Role: dense rhythmic layer (chaotic tone) but professional
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (punchy 60s loop, 109.96 BPM)
- Music treatment: full-energy bed at 0.34, ends with a short fade under the final logo hold.
- Music cue guidance: preset read from .agents/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10...json. Strong cues used: 3.5527s (hero line lock), 12.562s (97% payoff), 20.1898s (ZERIFY logo slam). Beat grid ~0.545s/beat — card/stat sequences snap to consecutive beats; readable text reveals hold ≥ 2 beats.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass RMS swells the purple hero glow and card presence. No waveform/equalizer visuals.

- Audio-coupled moments: score counter ticks, card-by-card discovery deal, verify click, escrow stamp, logo slam.
- Restraint rule: SFX never cover two consecutive beats with the same sound; every sound lands with a visible motion.
